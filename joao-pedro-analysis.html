Nobody Saw This Coming

At the start of the season nobody was talking about Joao Pedro as Chelsea's most important player. He looked soft, going down looking for easy fouls instead of putting the ball in the net.

From Soft to Drogba — The Transformation

Suddenly something clicked. He is now scoring goals that remind you of Didier Drogba in his prime — powerful, clinical, and unstoppable.

⚽ The Napoli Away Game — That Was The Moment

His performance away to Napoli secured Chelsea's place in the last 16 without needing play-offs.
